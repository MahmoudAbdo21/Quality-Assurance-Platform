# Logo Generation Report

## Visual Concept
The generated logo was designed to act as a professional flat-vector emblem representing an academic quality assurance platform in Egypt. 
It features minimalist symbols consisting of:
- **Academic Book:** Representing higher education and knowledge.
- **Quality Checkmark:** Denoting accreditation and rigorous standard enforcement.
- **Protective Shield:** Signifying safeguarding of academic integrity.

## Selected Colors
- **Primary:** Dark Green `#15803D`
- **Secondary:** Academic Gold `#FBBF24`
- **Accents:** White `#FFFFFF`

## Generated Asset Paths
- **Final PNG Path:** `public/brand/quality-accreditation-logo.png`
- **Favicon (32x32):** `public/brand/favicon-32.png`
- **Favicon (192x192):** `public/brand/favicon-192.png`
- **Apple Touch Icon:** `public/brand/apple-touch-icon.png`
- **Favicon (512x512):** `public/brand/favicon-512.png`

## Final Dimensions
The raw image was generated as `1024x1024`. It is downscaled safely in HTML to `68x68` on desktop, maintaining sharp rendering and optimal contrast against the green header.

## Why This Candidate Was Chosen
The design has a balanced visual weight, avoiding excessive complexity or distracting photographic/3D elements. It perfectly aligns with the institution's professional branding while scaling legibly down to mobile and favicon sizes.

## Affirmation of Independence
**Confirmation:** No official logo, Al-Azhar, Egyptian governmental seal, or other copyrighted mark was copied or reproduced. The design is a fully original, distinct vector-style composition.
